The rules
all six surfaces obey.

Six interfaces only read as one product if they share more than a logo. This page documents the decisions that make that true — the tokens, the type scale, the spacing, and the components that repeat across every screen. Everything below is the live CSS, not a picture of it.

Colour

Two decisions rule the palette: violet is the only accent, cyan only exists when a second variable is genuinely present.

Why violet is a single accent. The EF kit fills large panels with brand violet. Across six dense screens that would flatten every table into one tone, so the surface stays neutral and violet is reserved for primary actions, the active nav item, and the leading value in a comparison. A violet element always means: look here.

Why a mono face at all. On a risk board, aligned digits are a feature, not decoration. Proportional figures make a 12-row comparison scan unevenly; tabular mono figures let the eye run down a column. The kit's display face is excellent at poster size and costs legibility at 12px inside a sixty-row table, which is why body text is Inter rather than the display family.

Grid & spacing

One 72px engineering grid sits under the dark ground at 2% opacity. Every card, table cell and gutter derives from it.

72px grid · 2% white. Keeps large dark areas from reading as empty and makes card borders feel intentional.

Spacing scale 4 / 8 / 12 / 14 / 22 / 26 / 46 / 66. Cards use 22px padding, gaps 14px, section rhythm 66px, page gutter 28px (18px under 780px).

What this buys the six screens

Consistency

One navigation

Identical top bar on all six pages, same routes, same active state. A reader never has to relearn where things are.

Legibility

One type contract

Display is always Grotesk, prose always Inter, figures always mono. Numbers can be compared across screens without re-reading.

Meaning

One colour contract

Violet means look here, cyan means a second axis, green and red only ever mean pass and fail. No screen invents its own semantics.

Deliberate departures, restated. Violet as a single accent rather than a surface · a cool cyan added as a second signal for the two comparison screens · Space Grotesk with Inter and JetBrains Mono instead of the kit's single display face · a 72px grid at 2% under the dark ground. Each of these is explained where it appears on the page it affects.
