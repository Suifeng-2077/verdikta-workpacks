What it is not

Not a vote

Trading does not move anyone's submission up the list. It produces a number that a reviewer can look at, ignore, or argue with.

What it fixes

The silent tie-break

Selection rounds often come down to two entries and an unwritten preference. A price makes that preference visible before the decision, not after it.

Who is exposed

Traders carry the risk

If the market is wrong, traders lose, not the entrants. That is the reason a submission's price can be read without implicating the submission.
