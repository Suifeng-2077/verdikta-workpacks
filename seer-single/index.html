What the shared core actually settles

Three primitives are reused by every interface above. This is the reason the six surfaces can share one balance sheet.

Primitive

Conditional tokens

A position is a claim on a specific outcome. Split, merge, and redeem are uniform across categorical, scalar and futarchy markets.

Primitive

Resolution sources

Markets declare who resolves them and what happens if that source stays silent. The same dispute path applies from a five-option grant round to a token-price futarchy test.

Primitive

One collateral pool

Capital posted on Deep is available to Risk Pricing. Six interfaces draw on one pool, which is why comparing estimates across products is meaningful at all.
